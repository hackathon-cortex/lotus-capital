2,500+ clients · ₹50 crore+ AUM · 800+ demat accounts · 1,200+ mutual fund folios · ₹50 lakh+ fixed deposits · 250+ health, 180+ life, 140+ motor policies · 30+ properties brokered · ₹15 lakh+ monthly rent · approx. 23% equity CAGR (held) · AMFI, NSE and BSE authorised person, IRDAI, NPS and RERA entries.

## Content rules
1. Every figure shows an as-of date and comes from the register.
2. Figures are client-supplied until the client confirms them. Tier B figures need a definition; Tier C (23% CAGR) needs compliance approval, or it is omitted.
3. Never promise returns or imply past results predict future results.
4. Name the holder and role for every registration. Do not say "SEBI-registered" or "RERA registered" unless verified. RERA is "under process".
5. Show the mutual fund disclaimer in the footer of every public page.
6. Use confirmed partner logos and approved legal text only. Do not invent relationships, awards, testimonials, fees or performance.

## Workflow
Draft → client verifies figures and registrations → compliance review → client approves → publish through the CMS.
