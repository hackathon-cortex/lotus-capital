# Content Approval Checklist

Use before any page or statistic goes live.

## Business identity
- [ ] Legal entity and trading name confirmed.
- [ ] Service descriptions match current activities.
- [ ] Contact details verified (phone, email, address, WhatsApp).
- [ ] Leadership names, roles, bios and photographs approved.

## Statistics (see `00-client-data-register.md`)
- [ ] Each figure has an as-of date and a written definition.
- [ ] 2,500+ clients, 1,200+ folios, 800+ demat accounts: dates confirmed.
- [ ] 250+ health, 180+ life, 140+ motor policies: dates confirmed; derived 570+ total approved.
- [ ] ₹50 crore+ "AUM": segments included and terminology confirmed.
- [ ] ₹50 lakh+ fixed deposits: relation to the ₹50 crore+ total confirmed.
- [ ] 30+ properties and ₹15 lakh+ monthly rent: definitions and period confirmed.
- [ ] Counts say whether they are total or active accounts, policies or folios.
- [ ] No figure implies future returns.

## Performance claim (approx. 23% CAGR, equity)
- [ ] Period, method, basis, benchmark and costs documented.
- [ ] Supporting records reviewed.
- [ ] Compliance approval in writing.
- [ ] If any box above is unchecked, the claim is omitted.

## Disclaimers and legal
