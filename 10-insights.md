## Listing layout
Article cards: title, summary, category, read time, date. Pagination `?page=2`.

## Article standards
- Title, summary and last-updated date.
- Author or reviewer where approved.
- Sources and official links.
- Risk note where relevant.
- Related service and a soft enquiry call to action.
- No stock tips, guaranteed returns, forecasts without basis, invented statistics or copied text.
