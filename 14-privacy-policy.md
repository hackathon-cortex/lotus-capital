# Privacy Policy — `/privacy-policy` (Draft Outline)

Content outline only. The client or a legal reviewer must approve the final text before personal data is collected.

## Required topics
1. Legal entity and contact for privacy questions.
2. Data collected: name, mobile, email, service of interest, message, contact preference, source page and UTM data.
3. Purposes: responding to enquiries, follow-up, service delivery, security and legally required records.
4. Consent: the enquiry form includes a required consent tick box linked to this policy.
5. Who may receive data: hosting, database, email and CRM providers; insurers, brokers or fund houses only when the client asks to proceed.
6. Retention periods and deletion.
7. Security: encryption in transit, role-based access, audit logs, rate limiting.
8. User rights and how to make a request.
9. Cookies and analytics, if used.
10. Children's data, if relevant.
11. Cross-border processing and hosting region, if applicable.
12. Update date and contact channel.

## Data minimisation
The public site never collects passwords, OTPs, login credentials, PAN, Aadhaar or unnecessary financial details. CRM access depends on role. Client records and exports are limited to authorised staff.

## Inputs needed
Legal entity, privacy contact, hosting region, analytics tools, retention schedule, approved wording.
