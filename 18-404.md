# Not Found — `*`

## Purpose
Help visitors recover when a URL is invalid.

## Copy
**We couldn't find that page.**

The link may be outdated or the address may have been entered incorrectly. You can return to the homepage or browse our services.

## Actions
- Go Home → `/`
- Explore Services → `/services`
- Contact Us → `/contact-us`

Return a genuine 404 status where supported. Do not redirect every unknown URL to the homepage.
